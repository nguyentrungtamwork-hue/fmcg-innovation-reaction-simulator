# VerdeCalm Herbal Cool — Innovation Brief

**Brand (fictional):** VerdeCalm
**Category:** Ready-to-drink tea
**Launch market:** Vietnam

## Concept
VerdeCalm Herbal Cool is a ready-to-drink herbal tea with 50% less sugar than leading RTD teas,
built around a naturally cooling chrysanthemum-and-mint blend. It is positioned as a calm afternoon
reset for urban office workers who want refreshment without the sugar load.

## Target consumer
Urban office workers aged 22–35 in major Vietnamese cities. They drink bottled tea for refreshment
and a small pick-me-up, are increasingly sugar-aware, and shop convenience stores and supermarkets,
with growing social-commerce influence.

## Claims
- **Functional:** 50% less sugar than leading RTD teas; contains chrysanthemum and mint.
- **Emotional:** "A calm afternoon reset."

## Pack, price & channels
- 450ml frosted PET, slightly premium vs mainstream bottled tea.
- Convenience stores, supermarkets, social commerce.

## Go-to-market
- Short-form video creators and two KOLs.
- 4-week sampling at office-district convenience stores.
- Buy-one-get-one at convenience chains.

## Known risks
- Consumers may not believe the natural cooling claim.
- Premium price may suppress trial.
- Herbal taste may feel medicinal to some.

_Exploratory concept for simulation — fictional brand, not a real product._
