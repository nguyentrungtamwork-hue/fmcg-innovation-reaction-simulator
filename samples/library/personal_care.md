# PureLeaf Gentle Micellar Wash — Innovation Brief

**Brand (fictional):** PureLeaf
**Category:** Personal care
**Launch market:** United Kingdom

## Concept
PureLeaf Gentle Micellar Wash is a fragrance-free, pH-balanced micellar face & body wash for
sensitive skin. It cleanses without stripping, positioned on dermatologist-tested gentleness for
people whose skin reacts to harsher or fragranced products.

## Target consumer
Adults aged 20–45 with sensitive or reactive skin who cleanse morning and evening and after workouts,
and who shop pharmacies, supermarkets and online for trusted, gentle formulas.

## Claims
- **Functional:** Fragrance-free; pH-balanced; dermatologist-tested.
- **Emotional:** "Calm, reassured skin."

## Pack, price & channels
- 300ml recyclable pump bottle, mid-premium.
- Pharmacies, supermarkets, e-commerce.

## Go-to-market
- Dermatologist endorsements and explainer content.
- Pharmacy sampling and trial sizes.
- Trial-size cross-sell.

## Known risks
- Shoppers doubt that a fragrance-free wash can feel properly clean.
- Hard to stand out in a crowded sensitive-skin aisle.
- Claim trust requires visible proof.
