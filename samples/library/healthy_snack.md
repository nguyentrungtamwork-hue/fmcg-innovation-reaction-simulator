# CrispRoot Baked Veggie Crisps — Innovation Brief

**Brand (fictional):** CrispRoot
**Category:** Healthy snack
**Launch market:** Singapore

## Concept
CrispRoot Baked Veggie Crisps are oven-baked crisps made from real beetroot, carrot and parsnip,
with 40% less fat than fried crisps. The concept offers a satisfying crunch with less guilt for
health-leaning snackers who don't want to give up flavour.

## Target consumer
Health-conscious millennials aged 25–40 in urban areas who snack at their desks, after the gym, and
at home, and who read labels but still want taste and crunch.

## Claims
- **Functional:** Baked not fried; 40% less fat than fried crisps; made with real vegetables.
- **Emotional:** "Snack without the guilt."

## Pack, price & channels
- Resealable 100g matte pouch, premium snack tier.
- Supermarkets, health stores, e-commerce.

## Go-to-market
- Fitness micro-influencers and recipe content.
- Gym and office sampling.
- Multi-buy bundles online.

## Known risks
- Consumers expect baked snacks to taste worse than fried.
- Premium price vs mainstream crisps.
- 100g pouch may feel small for the price.
