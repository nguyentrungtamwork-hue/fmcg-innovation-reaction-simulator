# NutriMorning High-Protein Drinkable Yogurt — Innovation Brief

**Brand (fictional):** NutriMorning
**Category:** Dairy / nutrition
**Launch market:** Germany

## Concept
NutriMorning is a drinkable high-protein yogurt with 20g protein per bottle, no added sugar, and
live gut-friendly cultures. It is positioned as a convenient, nutritious breakfast for busy
professionals who skip or rush the morning meal.

## Target consumer
Busy professionals aged 25–45 who commute, train, or work long hours and want a quick, filling,
high-protein breakfast they can drink on the go. They shop supermarkets, convenience stores and online.

## Claims
- **Functional:** 20g protein per bottle; no added sugar; live gut-friendly cultures.
- **Emotional:** "Win the morning."

## Pack, price & channels
- 250ml grab-and-go bottle, premium breakfast tier.
- Supermarkets, convenience stores, e-commerce.

## Go-to-market
- Routine and productivity creators.
- Commuter-hub sampling at transit stations.
- Multipack introductory price.

## Known risks
- High-protein drinks can taste chalky.
- Changing established breakfast habits is hard.
- Premium price vs spoonable yogurt.
