# LumaGlow Vitamin C Day Serum — Innovation Brief

**Brand (fictional):** LumaGlow
**Category:** Beauty / skincare
**Launch market:** United States

## Concept
LumaGlow Vitamin C Day Serum is a stabilized 10% vitamin C serum that promises visibly brighter,
more even skin in four weeks, in a non-sticky fast-absorbing formula. It is positioned for
early-aging-concern consumers who want visible radiance from a trusted daytime step.

## Target consumer
Skincare-engaged consumers aged 25–40 who follow a morning routine, read ingredient lists, and buy
based on reviews and visible results. They shop e-commerce, beauty retailers and pharmacies.

## Claims
- **Functional:** Stabilized 10% vitamin C; visible radiance in 4 weeks; non-sticky fast-absorb formula.
- **Emotional:** "Glow you can see."

## Pack, price & channels
- 30ml amber glass dropper, premium masstige.
- E-commerce, beauty retailers, pharmacies.

## Go-to-market
- Skinfluencer reviews and before/after content.
- Deluxe-sample with online orders.
- Gift-with-purchase launch.

## Known risks
- Consumers doubt visible-results claims.
- Vitamin C serums can feel sticky or oxidize over time.
- Very crowded vitamin C category.
