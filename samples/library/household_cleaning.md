# EcoSwirl Plant-Based Surface Spray — Innovation Brief

**Brand (fictional):** EcoSwirl
**Category:** Household cleaning
**Launch market:** Australia

## Concept
EcoSwirl Plant-Based Surface Spray is a multi-surface cleaner with a plant-based formula and a
refillable system — a reusable bottle plus concentrate refill pouches. It promises effective
cleaning with less plastic and fewer harsh chemicals for eco-minded households.

## Target consumer
Eco-minded households aged 28–50 who want to reduce plastic and chemical use but still expect a
cleaner that genuinely works on kitchens and bathrooms. They shop supermarkets, eco stores and online.

## Claims
- **Functional:** Plant-based formula; 99.9% effective on common surfaces; refillable to reduce plastic.
- **Emotional:** "A cleaner home and a cleaner conscience."

## Pack, price & channels
- 500ml reusable bottle + concentrate refill pouch, premium vs mainstream sprays.
- Supermarkets, e-commerce, eco stores.

## Go-to-market
- Sustainability creators and how-to refill content.
- In-store starter-kit demos.
- Starter-kit bundle with the first refill free.

## Known risks
- Consumers fear plant-based cleaners are less effective than chemical ones.
- Refilling adds effort vs grab-and-replace.
- Premium price vs cheap sprays.
