## 13. Strategic Recommendations
- **[P0] Lead the launch with a clearer, substantiated reason-to-believe for the core claim.** (Brand) — 'Premium price vs mainstream alternatives' is the most frequent barrier and the main repeat constraint. _Evidence:_ Top barrier appears 68 times across segments. _Impact:_ Higher credibility → improved trial-to-repeat conversion.
- **[P0] Use sampling to de-risk taste before scaling paid media.** (Trade Marketing) — Sensory uncertainty (herbal taste) suppresses repeat and drives complaints. _Evidence:_ Post-trial complaints concentrate where taste importance is high. _Impact:_ Lower one-and-done risk; stronger word-of-mouth.
- **[P1] Focus initial targeting on the beachhead segments: Family Decision Maker, Promotion-Driven Trial Buyer.** (Media) — These segments show the highest simulated trial probability. _Evidence:_ Lead segment avg trial 0.538. _Impact:_ Efficient early trial and advocacy seeding.
- **[P1] Anchor trial on the sampling + BOGO mechanic rather than a price cut.** (Sales) — 20 consumers deferred to a promotion/sample before trialling — promo dependency is HIGH. _Evidence:_ A meaningful share of consumers deferred to a promo/sample. _Impact:_ Protects premium positioning while driving trial.
- **[P2] Secure visible facings and a strong e-commerce product page in convenience + TikTok Shop.** (E-commerce) — Findability gates the shelf/e-commerce round. _Evidence:_ 7 touchpoints analysed; trial concentrates where visibility is high. _Impact:_ Converts consideration into trial at the point of purchase.
- **[P2] Brief creators on a proof-of-taste, honest-review format.** (Creative) — Skeptical and review-dependent segments need credible third-party validation. _Evidence:_ request_review actions cluster in the comms round. _Impact:_ Builds credibility ahead of broad trial.
